FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does the adaptive mock simulator generate questions?

The Question Generator agent coordinates with RAG retriever models to fetch specific guidelines from our database based on your parsed target role, company, and projects. It then builds highly context-aware engineering prompts, ensuring you are tested on realistic benchmarks.

What makes the visual feedback superior to normal templates?

Rather than a basic numerical score, we output a granular scorecard mapping technical precision, communication style, and relevance, paired with structured bulleted items of your exact strengths, weakness gaps, and actionable improvement recommendations.
